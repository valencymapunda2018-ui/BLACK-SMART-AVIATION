Our Purpose

Our Mission. Our Future.

BLACK SMART AVIATION aims to connect Black aviation talent across Africa and the world, promote aviation knowledge and opportunities, and inspire young people to pursue careers in the aviation industry. We believe that talent, ambition and opportunity can open the skies to the next generation.
