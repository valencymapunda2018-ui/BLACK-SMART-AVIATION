Our Purpose

Building an Inclusive Aviation Community

Black Smart Aviation aims to connect Black aviation talent, inspire young people and promote access to aviation education, career awareness and industry knowledge.

We believe that ambition, talent and opportunity should be encouraged. By sharing knowledge, building connections and celebrating diverse aviation journeys, we can help inspire the next generation.

Join the Community Contact Us

Our Mission in Action

We aim to help make aviation more visible, understandable and welcoming to people who want to learn about the industry.

  • Promote aviation education and awareness
  • Encourage young people to explore aviation careers
  • Connect people with shared aviation interests
  • Share knowledge and inspiring experiences
  • Support inclusion and community participation
